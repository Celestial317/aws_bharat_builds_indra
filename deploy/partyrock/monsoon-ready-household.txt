PARTYROCK APP BLUEPRINT - "Monsoon-Ready Household"
Create at https://partyrock.aws  (free, no AWS account or card needed) -> "Build your own app" -> paste the app prompt.

APP PROMPT (paste into the generator)
-------------------------------------
Build a household flood-preparedness planner for India. The user enters a district or town, their roof
area in square metres, how many people live in the home, and whether they have a rainwater tank.
Show: (1) a 7-day monsoon prep checklist, (2) how much rainwater their roof will shed in a 100 mm
cloudburst (area x depth x 0.8 runoff, in litres) and where that water should go so the street does not
flood, (3) a short "what to do when the street floods" safety card, and (4) a chat box to ask follow-ups.

WIDGETS (if you prefer to build by hand)
----------------------------------------
User Input   "District"            placeholder: Pune
User Input   "Roof area (sq m)"    placeholder: 100
User Input   "People at home"      placeholder: 4
User Input   "Do you have a tank?" placeholder: no

Text         "Cloudburst volume"
  Prompt: Using a roof area of @Roof area (sq m) square metres and a 100 mm cloudburst, compute litres
  shed = area x 100 x 0.8. Explain in two sentences what that volume means for @District and the street
  outside (compare it with a tanker truck of 10,000 litres). Show the arithmetic.

Text         "7-day prep checklist"
  Prompt: Write a 7-day pre-monsoon checklist for a household of @People at home in @District who
  answered "@Do you have a tank?" to having a rainwater tank. Cover gutters, first-flush diverter,
  overflow to a recharge pit, electrical safety, stored drinking water (3 L per person per day for 5
  days), documents and medicines. Keep each day to one line.

Text         "Street flooding safety card"
  Prompt: A short, calm safety card for when water enters the street in @District: what to switch
  off, where to move valuables, which numbers to call (national emergency 112), and what not to do.

Chatbot      "Ask INDRA"
  Prompt: You are a practical rainwater-harvesting and flood-preparedness assistant for Indian
  households. The user lives in @District with a roof of @Roof area (sq m) square metres.
  Answer in under 90 words, no emoji, and say when you are unsure.

NOTE: PartyRock is a hosted AWS playground and cannot be self-hosted or called from code. INDRA's
production assistant is the Strands agent in backend/indra/ai/agents.py, which uses real tools
(climate engine, RWH sizing, document search) instead of prompt-only widgets.
